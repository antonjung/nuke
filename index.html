How to Play

  1. Take turns placing one counter on any empty cell or one you already own.
  2. When a cell is full it explodes — counters fly out to every neighbour and capture them.
  3. Chain reactions keep firing until the board is stable.
  4. Win by being the only player left with counters on the board.

Classic: cells explode when counters equal their orthogonal neighbours — corners at 2, edges at 3, inner at 4. Enhanced: all 8 surrounding cells count — corners at 3, edges at 5, inner at 8. Cells are round and electrons orbit when a cell is one away from exploding. In 3D mode tap a surface cell to place and drag to rotate — interior cells fill via chain reactions only. Demo mode lets the AI play both sides.
