How to Play

  1. Take turns placing one counter on any empty square or one you already own.
  2. When a square holds as many counters as it has neighbours it explodes — counters fly out to every neighbour.
  3. Captured neighbours change to your colour. Chain reactions keep going until everything is stable.
  4. You win by being the only player left with counters on the board.

Corner squares need only 2 counters to explode. Edge squares need 3. Inner squares need 4.
